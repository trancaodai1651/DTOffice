# Default skill: motion

## Workflow

- Plan timing, transitions, captions and the visual payoff before rendering.
- Use licensed assets, accessible captions and format-appropriate aspect ratios.
- Verify the exported video actually plays and report codec, dimensions and duration.

## Handoff

Deliver the requested files and a short report: decisions, evidence, checks actually run, remaining risks and next owner. Update the project handoff as its instructions require. Do not claim training, testing or execution that did not occur.

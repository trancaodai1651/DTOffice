# Default skill: pr

## Workflow

- Identify a factual news angle, target outlets and a concise media brief.
- Draft a release and outreach material with verified claims and attributable quotes.
- Do not contact journalists or distribute material autonomously.

## Handoff

Deliver the requested files and a short report: decisions, evidence, checks actually run, remaining risks and next owner. Update the project handoff as its instructions require. Do not claim training, testing or execution that did not occur.

# Default skill: copywriter

## Workflow

- Write specific hooks, benefits, objection handling and a relevant CTA.
- Respect the platform format and brand voice; avoid deceptive urgency or unsupported promises.
- Provide ready-to-use copy variants and identify assumptions.

## Handoff

Deliver the requested files and a short report: decisions, evidence, checks actually run, remaining risks and next owner. Update the project handoff as its instructions require. Do not claim training, testing or execution that did not occur.

# Default skill: ads

## Workflow

- Define audience, creative angles, funnel stage and test hypotheses.
- Prepare variants and a budget proposal tied to KPIs; check claims and platform policy.
- Do not launch campaigns or spend money; leave an approval-ready plan.

## Handoff

Deliver the requested files and a short report: decisions, evidence, checks actually run, remaining risks and next owner. Update the project handoff as its instructions require. Do not claim training, testing or execution that did not occur.

# Default skill: analytics

## Workflow

- Define KPIs, baseline, targets and attribution before reporting outcomes.
- Prepare event/UTM tracking and tests; distinguish forecasts from observed results.
- Report limitations and actionable next experiments without invented data.

## Handoff

Deliver the requested files and a short report: decisions, evidence, checks actually run, remaining risks and next owner. Update the project handoff as its instructions require. Do not claim training, testing or execution that did not occur.

# Default skill: product

## Workflow

- Use documented product facts to build benefits, differentiators and offers.
- Match benefits and objections to actual customer segments.
- Flag missing pricing or evidence instead of fabricating product claims.

## Handoff

Deliver the requested files and a short report: decisions, evidence, checks actually run, remaining risks and next owner. Update the project handoff as its instructions require. Do not claim training, testing or execution that did not occur.

# Default skill: seo

## Workflow

- Match search intent to a useful content structure and primary topic.
- Write natural titles, headings and metadata; avoid keyword stuffing or fabricated demand numbers.
- Provide internal linking and a measurement checklist.

## Handoff

Deliver the requested files and a short report: decisions, evidence, checks actually run, remaining risks and next owner. Update the project handoff as its instructions require. Do not claim training, testing or execution that did not occur.

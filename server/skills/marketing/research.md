# Default skill: research

## Workflow

- Research audience, demand and competitors using identifiable dated sources.
- Distinguish evidence, estimates and assumptions; avoid invented statistics.
- Deliver insights that directly affect the campaign brief.

## Handoff

Deliver the requested files and a short report: decisions, evidence, checks actually run, remaining risks and next owner. Update the project handoff as its instructions require. Do not claim training, testing or execution that did not occur.

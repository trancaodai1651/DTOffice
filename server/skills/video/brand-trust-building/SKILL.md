# Brand Trust Building

Default to Vietnamese output. Use this skill to design trust assets and trust-building content.

## Trust Framework

Use 9 ethical proof routes:

1. Familiarity through consistent presence.
2. RFT: real reviews, feedback handling, testimonials.
3. Power customers or recognizable customer proof.
4. Credible partners and supply-chain proof.
5. CAD: certificates, awards, diplomas, licenses, official documents.
6. Data and objective evidence.
7. Relevant influencers, KOLs, KOCs, experts.
8. Press and third-party media.
9. Transparent handling of negative feedback or public issues.

Read `references/trust-proof-reference.md` for details.

## Workflow

1. Identify the trust barrier: unknown brand, product efficacy doubt, price doubt, safety doubt, founder credibility, market skepticism, or past negative feedback.
2. Inventory existing proof.
3. Choose 3-5 proof assets to collect or publish.
4. Turn proof into content: post, landing-page block, short video, testimonial script, FAQ, sales message, or case study.
5. Add compliance checks.

## Guardrails

- Do not create fake reviews, fake orders, fake numbers, fake awards, or fake scandals.
- Do not overclaim medical, financial, legal, or safety outcomes.
- Separate verified facts from opinion or customer experience.
- Handle negative feedback with transparency and resolution, not manipulation.

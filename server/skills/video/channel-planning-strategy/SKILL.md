# Channel Planning Strategy

Default to Vietnamese output. Use this skill to turn business goals and audience insight into a practical channel plan.

## Planning Workflow

1. Clarify objective: brand awareness, leads, sales, recruitment, trust, community, or personal brand.
2. Analyze 5C: context, customer, competitor, collaborator/partner, company/self resources.
3. Segment and target: choose the audience slice that is valuable and reachable.
4. Position the channel with one memorable keyword or promise.
5. Map the content funnel:
   - awareness and like
   - trust and proof
   - conversion
   - customer care and fan building
6. Build 4-6 content pillars from audience pains, interests, trust barriers, and conversion needs.
7. Choose platform-specific formats: status, Reels, TikTok, carousel, photo post, livestream, case study, Q&A.
8. Create a 30-day test calendar.
9. Define stage-specific metrics.
10. Add resource constraints: who writes, who records, who edits, how many posts per week.

Read `references/channel-plan-template.md` when creating a full plan.

## Defaults

- Do not start with random content ideas. Start with goal, audience, platform, and resources.
- New channels should prove a repeatable concept before scaling production.
- Conversion becomes harder as the topic gets broader; widen reach only when the funnel can handle it.
- Treat content as the bridge between business/brand strategy and market execution.

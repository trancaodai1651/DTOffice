# Viral Content Concepts

Default to Vietnamese output. Use this skill to turn a topic, product, niche, audience, or business goal into practical viral content angles.

## Workflow

1. Identify the goal: reach, trust, conversion, community, recruitment, education, or retention.
2. Identify the audience insight: pain, desire, fear, curiosity, pride, shame, belief, objection, or social pressure.
3. Use key expansion before ideation: product key -> usage context -> people involved -> time/place -> conflict -> emotion -> proof -> story.
4. Select 3-5 concepts from `references/concept-reference.md` when the user needs many ideas or a serious plan.
5. Shape each idea with DCIK:
   - D: đối tượng/target viewer
   - C: concept truyền thông
   - I: insight
   - K: keyword, hook, or extra contextual trigger
6. Convert ideas into the requested format: Facebook status, Reels/TikTok script, carousel, livestream topic, content calendar, or campaign pillar.
7. Add a risk check when using hot topics, controversy, sensitive people, health, finance, children, accidents, or platform-policy themes.

## Output Pattern

For ideation, return a table:

| Concept | Hook/title | Core idea | Format | Why it can work | Risk/check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then recommend the first idea to test and explain why.

## Practical Defaults

- A viral angle should combine familiarity with difference.
- The first 3-5 seconds or first sentence must be concrete enough to stop scrolling.
- Do not choose a broad topic if the goal is conversion; narrow the audience and pain first.
- For new channels, prefer repeatable concepts over one-off tricks.
- If the user asks for "viral bằng mọi giá", reframe to high-reach but brand-safe concepts.

# Content Case Study Analysis

Default to Vietnamese output. Use this skill to analyze content channels and extract practical lessons.

## Analysis Workflow

1. Define the case: platform, niche, time period, objective, audience, and visible result.
2. Map the starting conditions: resources, timing, market context, existing proof, team, and constraints.
3. Identify positioning: who the channel is for, what keyword/promise repeats, why people should remember it.
4. Map content pillars: reach, trust, conversion, community.
5. Analyze winning assets: hook, format, topic, storytelling, proof, timing, distribution, comments, seeding.
6. Analyze failure points: unclear niche, weak first seconds, no repeatable concept, poor trust, poor conversion path, policy risk, resource mismatch.
7. Extract reusable mechanics, not surface copying.
8. Turn lessons into an action plan for the user's channel.

Read `references/case-study-template.md` and `references/case-examples.md` when doing full analysis.

## Output Template

```markdown
## Case summary
## Starting conditions
## Why it worked / failed
## Content mechanics
| Mechanic | Evidence | Lesson |
## What to copy
## What not to copy
## Action plan
```

## Guardrails

- Separate visible signals from hypotheses.
- Do not invent private metrics.
- Do not copy a creator's identity; adapt mechanics to the user's audience and resources.

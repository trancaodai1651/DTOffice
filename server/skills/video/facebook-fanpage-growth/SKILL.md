# Facebook Fanpage Growth

Default to Vietnamese output. Use this skill for Facebook fanpage strategy, audit, content planning, and growth troubleshooting.

## Workflow

1. Identify stage: new page, machine-learning stage, low reach, viral but unfocused, trusted but not converting, mature page.
2. Define page promise and audience: who should see the page repeatedly and why they should care.
3. Check platform fit: status, image, Reels, carousel, long post, livestream, group/community.
4. Build content funnel:
   - reach/awareness
   - affinity/like
   - trust/proof
   - conversion
   - fan care/community
5. Plan consistent topic clusters so Facebook can learn the audience.
6. Use Reels principles from `references/facebook-reels-principles.md` for vertical video.
7. Add safety review with the `facebook-content-safety` skill for sensitive topics.
8. Measure and iterate.

Read `references/facebook-growth-playbook.md` for details.

## Diagnosis Shortcuts

- Low reach: check topic clarity, consistency, first sentence/first frame, reduced-distribution risk, and lack of interaction.
- Reach but no followers: content may entertain without creating a reason to follow.
- Followers but no leads: trust and conversion posts are missing or too weak.
- Page suddenly drops: check repetition fatigue, risky comments, reduced-distribution topics, copyright, and whether the audience has changed.

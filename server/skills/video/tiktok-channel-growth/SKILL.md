# TikTok Channel Growth

Default to Vietnamese output. Use this skill for TikTok channel strategy, audit, and troubleshooting.

## Workflow

1. Identify stage: new account, testing, low views, viral one-offs, stable audience, weak conversion, or monetization.
2. Define one core niche and promise. Avoid mixing unrelated labels.
3. Map the audience TikTok should learn:
   - what they watch to completion
   - what they rewatch
   - what they save/share/comment
   - why they would follow
4. Choose content pillars:
   - reach hook
   - useful/educational
   - personality/trust
   - conversion/monetization
5. Design a 14-day or 30-day test with stable concept variables.
6. Diagnose by metrics: retention, average watch time, completion, rewatch, shares, saves, comments, follower conversion, profile clicks.
7. Create next tests: hook, topic, format, length, series, CTA, carousel.

Read `references/tiktok-growth-notes.md` and `references/tiktok-photo-carousel.md` for details.

## Diagnosis Shortcuts

- Low views: unclear niche, weak first 3 seconds, risky/policy issue, too few posts, wrong format for audience.
- Views but no followers: no repeatable promise or identity.
- Followers but no sales: trust/conversion content is missing or audience mismatch exists.
- Viral one-off: create a series and anchor it to the niche before chasing a new concept.
- Plateau: keep the winning concept but update topics, depth, packaging, and series logic.

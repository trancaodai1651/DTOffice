# Storytelling Million Views

Default to Vietnamese output. Use this skill to make stories concrete, emotional, and readable without becoming melodramatic.

## Core Formula

1. Open with one short sentence that enters the event and emotion directly.
2. Build the body with context, characters, timeline, tension, and a visible turning point.
3. Detail the body with scene, dialogue, inner thought, sensory cues, and specific actions.
4. End with a personal viewpoint, lesson, regret, gratitude, or reflective meaning.

Read `references/storytelling-formula.md` for the detailed structure.

## Rewrite Workflow

1. Identify the emotional center: fear, shame, pride, regret, gratitude, love, loss, ambition, relief, or anger.
2. Pick the story type: journey, before/after, cause-effect, first time, failure, proof, emotional moment, or brand origin.
3. Create the first sentence as action plus emotion.
4. Arrange the body chronologically unless a flashback creates stronger curiosity.
5. Add one concrete detail per beat. Replace generic feelings with visible evidence.
6. Put the climax near the late-middle, then close with a short reflection.
7. Remove moralizing lines that the story has already proven.

## Output Modes

If the user gives a rough story, return:

- Phiên bản viết lại
- 3 hook thay thế
- Chỗ đã tăng cảm xúc
- Chỗ còn thiếu dữ liệu thật

If the user gives only a topic, return:

- 3 story angles
- Recommended angle
- Complete draft

## Guardrails

- Do not fake trauma or claim fictional events are real.
- Protect minors, victims, private individuals, and sensitive identities.
- Avoid graphic details for self-harm, sexual violence, accidents, or illness.
- Make emotional content useful or meaningful, not merely shocking.

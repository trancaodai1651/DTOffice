# DT Office 0.4.7

- A meeting before every piece of work: when a stage of the AI Agent Dev team starts, everyone taking part gathers at the meeting table with the director, who opens it; each takes their part in turn, then they go to their desks.
- The news reader and the video editor meet the same way (together, when the video is made from a news report), work at a desk, and go back to the library when the job is done.
- The Codex tab of the plan card shows "What eats your plan" with today's Codex tokens and the model eating most; it opens the Codex view of the drawer.
- A stale first read of the specialists' jobs no longer overwrites newer news from the live stream.

Download the Windows `-setup.exe` or macOS `_universal.dmg`. The `.app.tar.gz` contains the same Mac application. Verify downloads using `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

The macOS app is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized. Windows installers are unsigned. Existing app data stays in `~/.my-office`. AI services require a separate account login. The video editor needs ffmpeg (winget on Windows, Homebrew on macOS).

The MIT copyright and permission notices remain included in the source and packaged app.

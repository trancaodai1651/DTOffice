# DT Office 0.4.6

- News reader: give a topic; it searches Vietnamese and foreign press and returns a sourced summary as a PDF (printed with the computer's Edge or Chrome), then offers to turn it into a video.
- Video editor: plans and cuts a video with nine bundled content skills (short-video scripts, storytelling, hooks, viral concepts, platform growth, content safety, brand trust) and ffmpeg; footage found on the web (free-to-use, credited) or from a folder of yours, saved where you choose. A button installs ffmpeg when it is missing.
- Codex Bot: a small blue robot that floats around the office while Codex is one of your CLIs; click it for Codex's limits.
- The guide (Mai) takes work: she asks which project and picks the AI Agent team(s) for it; your own project list to add to and remove from.
- AI Agent Dev team: members shared across tasks wait for their current task, come to the director to take their part, stay in the office, rest in the lounge; going out needs your permission.
- "What eats your plan" for Codex too: tokens by model, member, project, session and day, with the 5-hour and weekly limits.

Download the Windows `-setup.exe` or macOS `_universal.dmg`. The `.app.tar.gz` contains the same Mac application. Verify downloads using `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

The macOS app is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized. Windows installers are unsigned. Existing app data stays in `~/.my-office`. AI services require a separate account login. The video editor needs ffmpeg (winget on Windows, Homebrew on macOS).

The MIT copyright and permission notices remain included in the source and packaged app.
